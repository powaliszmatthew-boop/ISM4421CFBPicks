Summary

Profit counts settled bets only: a win pays your stake times the odds, a loss costs the stake, and pushes and voids return it. Bets tracked from the Game lines and Player props pages settle themselves once ESPN posts the final score or stat line.

Add a bet

For bets you placed on your own. The Track buttons on the Game lines and Player props pages add a bet with the details filled in, and they settle automatically. For a parlay, use + Parlay on any pick (or Add as parlay leg below), then open the Parlay slip at the bottom left.

All bets

Change odds, stake, or result right in the table. It saves as soon as you change it. A parlay lists its legs; set a leg's result and the parlay updates: any lost leg loses it, and pushed or voided legs drop out of the payout.
